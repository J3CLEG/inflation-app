How to read this

These are arithmetic scenarios, not forecasts. Each one holds prices to a fixed monthly pace and shows the 12-month rate that results. Last winter's index was low, held down by the shutdown and cheap gas. So the 12-month rate climbs into early 2027 even at the calm pre-war pace, then drops in March 2027 when the March 2026 gas spike leaves the comparison.
